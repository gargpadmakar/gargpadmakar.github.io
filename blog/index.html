About me

Padmakar Garg is an Android developer who has spent eight-plus years shipping production apps, from e-commerce and CRM to news, astrology and secure messaging.

Since August 2022 he has been a Senior Android Developer at Sveltetech Technology, working on real-time messaging, WebRTC voice and video calling, and end-to-end encrypted communication.

Outside client work he publishes open-source Android libraries and writes on Medium and dev.to about system design, mobile security and CI/CD. He holds a B.Tech in Information Technology (GBTU).
